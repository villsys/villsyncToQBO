Privacy Policy for VilSync To QBO

Last Updated: April 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Joselito Villarta, CPA, MBA ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VilSync To QBO application. We collect information to provide and improve our services to you. This includes:

  • Authentication Data: Your Google account email used to log into our system.
  • QuickBooks Online (QBO) Data: OAuth tokens required to securely connect to your QBO account.
  • Transaction Data: Uploaded CSV reports (e.g., Amazon, Shopify). This data is processed temporarily in your browser and backend to facilitate the transfer to QBO.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your access to the VilSync application.
  • Format, map, and transmit your uploaded e-commerce transactions directly into your connected QuickBooks Online account.
  • Enforce usage limits and manage subscriptions.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or financial data. Data is only shared with Intuit (QuickBooks Online) based on your explicit authorization to facilitate the transaction sync. Our backend infrastructure is securely hosted on Google Firebase.

4. Data Retention and Security

Uploaded CSV files are processed and then discarded or stored temporarily in secure cloud storage. OAuth tokens are encrypted. You may disconnect your QBO account at any time, which revokes our access to your Intuit data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
